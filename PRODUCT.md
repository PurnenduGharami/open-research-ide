# Product

Open Research IDE is a local-first scientific research workspace for researchers who need an auditable path from curiosity to testable experiment. It combines a persistent research conversation, real literature, independent critique, experimental planning, lab outreach, results, and reproducible exports.

## Primary job
Start with a research question and leave with a synchronized, inspectable body of evidence, independent critiques, a practical experiment, and exportable research journey.

## Non-negotiables
Real OpenClaw/Cerebras inference and live OpenAlex/Semantic Scholar sources; never silently fabricate research artifacts. Dedicated Elephant plus three zero-memory Goldfish reviewers. Apple-style light UI with precision and restraint. Server-only secrets. Canonical state is the source for every count and view.

## Approved visual brief
“Clean Apple-style light-mode dashboard (white/soft-gray surfaces, hairline borders, crisp typography, muted status pills—no dark neon or purple AI styling). Put 80% of UI polish into the main Research canvas; keep secondary sidebar tabs as lightweight filtered views.”

## MVP success
A live demo can complete the full arc and export its actual state without setup pollution, mocked scientific content, build errors, or misleading telemetry.
